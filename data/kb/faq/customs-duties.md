# International customs duties

## Who pays duties?
Import duties and taxes on international orders are set by the destination country and are paid by the customer, usually to the carrier on delivery.

## Are duties refunded on returns?
No. Duties and import taxes are not refunded by Kestrel Outfitters, including when an international order is returned.

## Delays in customs
Customs can add a few days to the 7–14 business day international delivery time.
