# How do I track my order?

## Tracking link
When your order ships we email a shipping confirmation with a tracking link. You can also find tracking under **Orders** in your Kestrel account.

## Order statuses
- **Processing** — we are preparing your order. It can still be changed or cancelled.
- **Shipped** — the order is with the carrier.
- **Delivered** — the carrier reports delivery.

## Tracking hasn't updated
Tracking can pause for a day or two in transit. If a domestic package shows no movement for 7 days, contact us and we open a carrier investigation.
