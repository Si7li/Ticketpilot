# Gift Cards

## Buying gift cards
Digital gift cards are available from $25 to $500 and are delivered by email.

## Using gift cards
Enter the gift card code at checkout. Any remaining balance stays on the card for future orders. Gift cards do not expire.

## Refunds on gift card purchases
Orders paid with a gift card are refunded to the gift card balance or as store credit. Gift cards themselves cannot be refunded for cash.
