# Using store credit

## Where to find it
Store credit appears in your Kestrel account as soon as it is issued.

## Using it
Store credit is applied automatically at checkout when you are signed in. It can be combined with other payment methods.

## Expiry
Store credit does not expire. It cannot be exchanged for cash.
