# Updating your email address

## Change it yourself
Go to **Account settings → Email** and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address.

## Can't access the old email?
Contact support from any address with your name and a recent order ID. For your security we may ask extra questions. If you did not request an email change, see our Privacy and Account Security policy.
