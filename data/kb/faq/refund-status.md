# Where is my refund?

## Timing
Refunds to the original payment method take 5–7 business days after approval. Store credit is instant.

## Still not there?
Banks can take a few extra days to post a credit. If it has been longer than 7 business days since we confirmed your refund, contact us with your order ID.
