# Accepted payment methods

## What we accept
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Kestrel gift cards, and store credit.

## When you are charged
Your payment method is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships.

## Payment declined
If your payment is declined, check the billing address matches your card and try again, or use a different payment method. We cannot see why a bank declined a card.
