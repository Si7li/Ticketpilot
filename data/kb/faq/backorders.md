# Backorders and out-of-stock items

## Out-of-stock notifications
Choose **Notify me** on a product page to get an email when a size or color is back in stock.

## Backordered items
If part of your order is backordered, we ship the available items first and the rest when they arrive. You are charged for each item when it ships.
