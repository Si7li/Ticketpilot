# Final Sale Items

## What final sale means
Items marked **final sale** on the product page and on your order confirmation are sold as-is. Final sale items are **not returnable** and are not eligible for a refund or exchange.

## How to tell if an item is final sale
Final sale items show a "Final Sale" label next to the price at checkout and on the order confirmation email. Clearance and sample-sale events usually contain final sale items.

## Damaged or wrong final sale items
If a final sale item arrives damaged, or we shipped the wrong item, please contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order ID and a photo. These cases are reviewed individually by a specialist on our team, who will follow up by email.

## Final sale is different from sale
A regular sale item (discounted but not marked final sale) can still be returned, but it is refunded as store credit. See the Sale Items policy.
