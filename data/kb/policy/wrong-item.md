# Wrong Item Received

## Reporting window
If you received a different item from the one you ordered (wrong product, color, or size), please report it within **14 days of delivery**.

## What we need from you
Your order ID and a description or photo of the item you received, including the label on the packaging if possible.

## What we do
We ship the correct item to you **free of charge** as a replacement, and we email a **prepaid return label** for the wrong item. Please send the wrong item back within 14 days of receiving the label.

## If the correct item is out of stock
If we cannot ship the correct item, we refund it to your original payment method once the wrong item is on its way back.
