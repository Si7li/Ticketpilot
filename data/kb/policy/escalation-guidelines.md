# Escalation Guidelines

## Tickets that must be escalated
The following are always handed to a specialist team and never resolved by the front-line agent:
- **Chargebacks** or disputes filed or threatened with a bank → billing team.
- **Legal threats** or mentions of lawyers or lawsuits → legal team.
- **Press or media inquiries** → general team (communications).
- **Injury or safety incidents** involving our products → trust and safety team.
- **Harassment** of staff or customers → trust and safety team.
- **Suspected account takeover** → trust and safety team.

## What the customer is told
Thank the customer, confirm that their message has been passed to a specialist team, and say that the team will follow up by email. Do not promise outcomes, refunds, or timelines.

## Requests our policies do not cover
If a customer asks for something our policies do not cover, do not invent an answer. Tell the customer that a specialist will follow up.
