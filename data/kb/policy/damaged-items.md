# Damaged Item Policy

## Reporting window
If your order arrives damaged, please report it within **14 days of delivery**. Reports made after 14 days are handled under our Warranty policy if the item is covered.

## What we need from you
Please send your order ID and **a photo** that clearly shows the damage, and of the packaging if it was also damaged. We cannot process a damaged-item claim without a photo.

## Your options
Once we confirm the damage you choose between a **full refund** or a **replacement** of the damaged item. No restocking fee applies.

## Returning the damaged item
Kestrel may ask you to send the damaged item back. If so, we email you a **prepaid return label**. Please do not throw the item away until we confirm whether we need it back; the item is not automatically yours to keep.
