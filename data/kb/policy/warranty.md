# Warranty

## What is covered
Kestrel Outfitters tents, backpacks, and sleeping bags have a **2-year warranty** from the delivery date against **manufacturing defects** — for example failed seams, delaminating fabric, or broken zippers that were not caused by misuse.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, and damage from misuse or improper care are not covered. The warranty does not cover items damaged during delivery — please use the Damaged Item policy for that, within 14 days of delivery.

## How to make a warranty claim
Send us your **order ID** and **a photo** of the defect. Our team reviews the claim and offers a repair, replacement, or refund depending on the defect.
