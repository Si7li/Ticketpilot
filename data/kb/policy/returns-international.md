# International Returns Policy

This policy applies to orders shipped to an address outside the United States.

## Return window
You can return most items within **14 days of delivery**. The 14-day window starts on the delivery date shown in your order tracking, not the order date. Requests made after 14 days from delivery cannot be accepted as returns.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in their original condition, and with all original tags attached. Gear that has been used on a trip, washed, or altered cannot be accepted under the standard return policy.

## Return shipping costs
For international returns the customer pays return shipping. We recommend a tracked service, because we can only refund items that reach our warehouse. Customs duties and import taxes paid on the original delivery are not refunded by Kestrel Outfitters.

## Items that cannot be returned
Items marked **final sale** cannot be returned. Sale items that are not final sale are refunded as store credit. Opened electronics carry a restocking fee.

## How your refund is paid
Once your return is received and inspected, the refund goes back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days, or instantly as store credit if you prefer.
