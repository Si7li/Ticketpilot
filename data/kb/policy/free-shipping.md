# Free Shipping

## Who qualifies
Domestic orders **over $75** ship free with standard shipping. The total is calculated after discounts and before taxes.

## Express shipping
Express shipping is not included in free shipping, except for members of our Summit and Peak loyalty tiers, who get free express shipping.

## International orders
Free shipping does not apply to international orders.
