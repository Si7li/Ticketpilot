# Price Adjustments

## Price drops after you buy
If the price of an item you bought drops within **14 days of purchase**, we refund the difference as **store credit**.

## How to request
Contact us with your order ID and the item. The 14 days are counted from the order date.

## Limits
Price adjustments are issued as store credit only and are not available for final sale items.
