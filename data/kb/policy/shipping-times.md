# Shipping Times

## Domestic shipping
- **Standard:** 3–5 business days after the order ships.
- **Express:** 1–2 business days after the order ships.

## International shipping
International orders arrive in **7–14 business days** after the order ships. Customs processing can add time.

## When orders ship
Orders are usually prepared and shipped within 1–2 business days of being placed. You receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking link when your order ships.
