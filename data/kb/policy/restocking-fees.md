# Restocking Fees

## Which items carry a restocking fee
A **10% restocking fee** applies to **opened electronics**. At Kestrel Outfitters this means GPS units (such as the Trailmark GPS) and headlamps with rechargeable batteries (such as the Beacon 600R). Headlamps that run on standard AAA batteries are not considered electronics for this policy.

## How the fee is applied
The fee is 10% of the item price and is deducted from the refund. For example, an opened GPS unit bought for $299.00 is refunded at $269.10. Unopened electronics in sealed packaging are refunded in full.

## When the fee does not apply
No restocking fee is charged when the item arrived damaged, when we shipped the wrong item, or when the item is defective and covered by warranty.
