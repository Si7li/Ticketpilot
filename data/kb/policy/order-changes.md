# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## When changes are possible
You can change or cancel an order only while its status is **processing**. Once an order has **shipped**, it can no longer be changed or cancelled.

## What can be changed
While processing, we can change the shipping address, the shipping method, item sizes or colors, or cancel items or the whole order.

## After the order has shipped
If your order has already shipped, you can return items under our returns policy once they arrive.
