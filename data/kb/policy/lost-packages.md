# Lost Packages

## When a package counts as lost
For domestic orders, if tracking has shown **no movement for 7 days**, we treat the package as possibly lost and open an investigation with the carrier.

## Carrier investigation
The carrier investigation takes up to **10 business days**. We handle it for you; you do not need to contact the carrier yourself.

## Reship or refund
If the carrier has not found the package after 10 business days, we either **reship** your order at no cost or **refund** it to your original payment method — your choice.

## Packages marked delivered but not received
Please check with neighbors and your building's mail room, and wait one business day, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. Then contact us with your order ID.
