# Double Charges and Pending Authorizations

## Pending authorizations
When you place an order, your bank may show a pending authorization. If an order is changed or a payment is retried, you may briefly see two pending amounts. Pending authorizations that are not captured **disappear within 3–5 business days** on their own.

## Confirmed duplicate charges
If you see two **posted** (not pending) charges for the same order, that is a confirmed duplicate charge. Send us your order ID and the dates of both charges, and we **refund the duplicate charge** to your original payment method.

## Chargebacks
If you are considering a chargeback with your bank, please contact us first so we can resolve the issue directly.
