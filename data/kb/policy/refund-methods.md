# Refund Methods and Timing

## Refund to the original payment method
Refunds to your original payment method (card, PayPal, or Apple Pay) are processed within **5–7 business days**. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit on your statement.

## Store credit
Store credit is issued **instantly** and appears in your Kestrel account right away. Store credit does not expire.

## Which method applies
You can usually choose either method. Sale items are refunded as store credit only. Price adjustments are always issued as store credit.
