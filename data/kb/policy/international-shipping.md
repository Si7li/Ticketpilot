# International Shipping

## Where we ship
We ship to most countries. International orders arrive in 7–14 business days after shipping.

## Duties and taxes
Import duties and taxes are charged by the destination country and paid by the customer on delivery. They are not refunded by Kestrel Outfitters.

## Returns from abroad
International orders follow the International Returns policy: 14 days from delivery, customer pays return shipping.
