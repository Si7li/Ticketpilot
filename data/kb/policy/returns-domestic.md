# Domestic Returns Policy

This policy applies to orders shipped to an address in the United States.

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The 30-day window starts on the delivery date shown in your order tracking, not the order date. Members of our Peak loyalty tier get an extended 60-day return window on domestic orders (see the Loyalty Program page).

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in their original condition, and with all original tags attached. Gear that has been used on a trip, washed, or altered cannot be accepted under the standard return policy. If you believe your item is defective, please see our Warranty policy or our Damaged Item policy instead.

## Return shipping costs
For domestic returns the customer pays return shipping. The exception is a defective item: if the product is defective we cover the return shipping and send a prepaid label.

## Items that cannot be returned
Items marked **final sale** cannot be returned. Sale items that are not final sale can be returned but are refunded as store credit. Opened electronics carry a restocking fee.

## How your refund is paid
When you request a return within the window, we set up your refund right away. Refunds to the original payment method arrive within 5–7 business days; store credit is instant. Please send the item back unused with tags attached so the return can be completed.
