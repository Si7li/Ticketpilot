# Sale Items Returns

## Can I return a sale item?
Yes. Sale items that are **not** marked final sale can be returned within the normal return window (30 days from delivery for domestic orders, 14 days for international orders) if they are unused with tags attached.

## How sale items are refunded
Sale items are refunded as **store credit only**. We cannot refund sale items to a credit card or the original payment method. Store credit is issued instantly once the return is approved and can be used on any future order.

## Sale vs final sale
Sale means the item was discounted. Final sale means the item cannot be returned at all. Check your order confirmation: final sale items carry a separate "Final Sale" label.
