# Refund Review Process

## Refunds up to $100
Refunds of **$100 or less** are approved automatically once the request meets our policies.

## Refunds over $100
Refunds of **more than $100** are reviewed by a member of our support team before they are approved. While the review is in progress, we tell the customer that the refund is being reviewed — not that it has been processed or confirmed.

## What customers are told
Say that the refund request has been received and is being reviewed by our team, and that they will receive an email once it is approved. Do not promise a refund date before approval.
