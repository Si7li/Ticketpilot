# Loyalty Program

## Tiers
- **Trail** — every customer, from $0 spent.
- **Summit** — customers who spend **$500 or more per year**. Benefit: free express shipping.
- **Peak** — customers who spend **$1,500 or more per year**. Benefits: free express shipping and **60-day returns**.

## How tiers are calculated
Your tier is based on your spend over the past 12 months and updates automatically.

## Peak returns
The 60-day return window replaces the standard 30-day window on domestic orders. All other return conditions still apply.
