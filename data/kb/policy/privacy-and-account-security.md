# Privacy and Account Security

## Sharing order information
We only share order details with the account holder, using the email address on the account. We never confirm whether an order exists to anyone else.

## Suspected account takeover
If you notice orders you did not place, a changed password, or changed email, contact us immediately. Suspected account takeover is escalated to our trust and safety team.

## We never ask for
Kestrel staff will never ask for your password or full card number by email.

## Your data
You can ask us to export or delete your personal data at any time from your account settings or by contacting support.
