# Basecamp 4 Tent

## Capacity and season
The Basecamp 4 is a **4-person, 3-season** car-camping tent with near-vertical walls and 1.8 m peak height.

## Weight
Packed weight is **4.8 kg**. It is designed for car camping, not backpacking.

## Setup
Freestanding with three poles. Two people can pitch it in about 10 minutes.

## Price
$329.00.
