# Ridgeline 2 Tent

## Capacity and season
The Ridgeline 2 is a **2-person, 3-season** backpacking tent for spring, summer, and fall. Floor area is 2.7 m² with two doors and two vestibules.

## Weight
Trail weight is **1.9 kg** (4 lb 3 oz); packed weight with stakes and stuff sacks is 2.1 kg.

## Setup
Freestanding hubbed pole set with color-coded clips. Most people pitch it in under 5 minutes. The rainfly can be pitched first in the rain using the optional Ridgeline 2 footprint.
