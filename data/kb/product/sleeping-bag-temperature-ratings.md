# Understanding Sleeping Bag Temperature Ratings

## Comfort vs limit
- **Comfort rating:** the temperature at which a typical cold sleeper stays comfortable in a relaxed position.
- **Limit rating:** the lowest temperature at which a typical warm sleeper stays warm curled up. It is not a comfortable sleeping temperature.

The number in a bag's name (for example the 20 in Cirrus 20°) is its limit rating.

## Choosing a bag
Pick a bag whose comfort rating is at or below the coldest night you expect. Use a sleeping pad: the ground steals heat faster than air.
