# Choosing a Tent: Capacity and Seasons

## Capacity ratings
A tent's person rating assumes standard sleeping pads side by side with little room for gear. If you want space for gear or a dog, size up — for example a Ridgeline 3 for two people.

## 3-season vs 4-season
3-season tents (Ridgeline 2, Ridgeline 3, Talus 1, Basecamp 4) are for spring, summer, and fall. 4-season tents (Aspen 2) handle snow loads and winter wind.

## Footprints
A footprint protects the tent floor and extends its life. Each tent has a matching footprint.
