# Aspen 2 Four-Season Tent

## Capacity and season
The Aspen 2 is a **2-person, 4-season** mountaineering tent built for snow loads and strong wind.

## Weight
Trail weight is **2.9 kg**.

## Setup
Freestanding crossing-pole design with internal pole clips so it can be pitched from inside in bad weather.
