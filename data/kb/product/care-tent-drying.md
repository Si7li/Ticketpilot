# Care Guide: Drying and Storing Tents

## Drying
Never store a tent wet. Pitch it or hang it at home until the fly, body, and footprint are completely dry, which prevents mildew and coating damage.

## Cleaning
Sponge clean with cold water and mild soap. Never machine wash or dry a tent.

## Storage
Store loosely in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight.
