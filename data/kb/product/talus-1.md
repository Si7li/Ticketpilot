# Talus 1 Tent

## Capacity and season
The Talus 1 is a **1-person, 3-season** ultralight tent with a single door.

## Weight
Trail weight is **1.2 kg**.

## Setup
Semi-freestanding: it needs two stakes at the foot to stand. Pitch time is about 3 minutes.

## Price
$149.00.
