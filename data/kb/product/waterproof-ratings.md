# What Waterproof Ratings Mean

## Hydrostatic head (mm)
The mm number is how tall a column of water the fabric holds before leaking. 10,000 mm handles moderate rain; 20,000 mm handles heavy, sustained rain.

## Breathability (g/m²/24h)
How much moisture vapor passes out through the fabric per day. Higher is more breathable.

## Keeping a jacket waterproof
Dirt and oils reduce performance. See our guide to re-proofing waterproof jackets.
