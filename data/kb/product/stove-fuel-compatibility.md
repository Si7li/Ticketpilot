# Stove Fuel Compatibility

## Canister stoves
The Kindle Canister Stove works only with threaded isobutane-propane canisters. It is not compatible with butane-only "lindal" or puncture canisters, or with propane cylinders.

## Liquid-fuel stoves
The Kindle Multi-Fuel Stove runs on white gas or kerosene. It does not run on gasoline from a car.

## Flying with fuel
Fuel canisters and fuel cannot be packed in checked or carry-on luggage.
