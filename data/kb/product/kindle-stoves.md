# Kindle Stoves

## Kindle Canister Stove
Compact stove for **isobutane-propane canisters** with standard threaded (EN 417) valves. Boils 1 L of water in about 4 minutes. $59.00.

## Kindle Multi-Fuel Stove
Burns **white gas** and **kerosene** from a refillable fuel bottle. Best for cold weather and international travel. $139.00.
