# How to Measure Your Torso for a Backpack

## Measuring
Ask a friend to measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck (C7 vertebra) down to the line between the tops of your hip bones. That length is your torso length.

## Picking a size
Torso length decides pack size, not your height. Cascade sizes: S 15–17 in, M 17–19 in, L 19–21 in. Between sizes, choose the larger size.
