# Care Guide: Washing Down Gear

## Washing
Wash down jackets and sleeping bags (Ember, Cirrus) in a front-loading machine on a gentle cold cycle with a down-specific wash. Do not use fabric softener.

## Drying
Tumble dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls to break up clumps. Drying a sleeping bag can take several hours; make sure it is fully dry before storing.

## Storage
Store down bags loose in a large storage sack, not compressed.
