# Boot Sizing and Break-in

## Finding your size
Measure your feet in the evening while wearing hiking socks. You should have about a thumb's width between your longest toe and the end of the boot.

## Break-in
Wear new boots around the house and on short walks for 1–2 weeks before a long hike. Wearing boots indoors keeps them in returnable, unused condition.

## Hot spots
Hot spots usually mean the lacing needs adjusting or the size is wrong. Try a heel-lock lacing.
