# Cascade 50 and Cascade 65 Backpacks

## Capacity
- **Cascade 50** — 50 L, for 2–4 night trips, $229.00.
- **Cascade 65** — 65 L, for longer trips or winter loads, $269.00.

## Torso sizes
Both packs come in S (15–17 in), M (17–19 in), and L (19–21 in) torso lengths, with an adjustable hip belt.

## Load
Comfortable carrying up to 18 kg (Cascade 50) and 23 kg (Cascade 65).
