# Jacket Sizing Chart

## Men's chest measurements
S 35–37 in · M 38–40 in · L 41–43 in · XL 44–46 in · XXL 47–49 in

## Women's chest measurements
XS 31–32 in · S 33–34 in · M 35–36 in · L 37–39 in · XL 40–42 in

## Between sizes?
Shells (Squall, Squall Lite) are cut to fit over layers, so choose your usual size. For a closer fit on the Ember Down Jacket, size down if between sizes.
