# Care Guide: Re-proofing Waterproof Jackets

## When to re-proof
When water stops beading on the outside of your Squall or Squall Lite jacket and the fabric looks soaked, the durable water repellent (DWR) finish needs renewing.

## How
Wash the jacket with a technical cleaner, then apply a spray-on or wash-in DWR treatment. Tumble dry on low heat for 20 minutes to reactivate the finish.
