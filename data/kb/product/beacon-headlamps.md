# Beacon Headlamps (300 and 600R)

## Beacon 300
300 lumens, runs on **3 AAA batteries** (included). $34.95. Not considered electronics for restocking fees.

## Beacon 600R
600 lumens, built-in **rechargeable lithium-ion battery**, charges via USB-C in about 3 hours. $59.95. As a rechargeable headlamp, an opened Beacon 600R carries a 10% restocking fee if returned.

## Battery tips
Lithium AAA batteries work in the Beacon 300 and perform better in the cold.
