# Ridgeline 3 Tent

## Capacity and season
The Ridgeline 3 is a **3-person, 3-season** backpacking tent for spring, summer, and fall. Floor area is 3.9 m² with two doors and two vestibules.

## Weight
Trail weight is **2.4 kg** (5 lb 5 oz); packed weight with stakes and stuff sacks is 2.7 kg.

## Setup
Freestanding hubbed pole set with color-coded clips, the same system as the Ridgeline 2 with one extra cross pole. The Ridgeline 3 footprint is sold separately.

$239.00.
